1. Where the target spot is, from my POV. (GeoMath)
  - From the coordinates, determines:
  - true bearing, clockwise from true north
  - distance, using the haversine formula
  - elevation angle, atan2(spotHeight − yourHeight, distance)

2. Where the phone is pointing. (CameraOrientation)
  - Uses the accelerometer (which points toward gravity) and magnetometer:
  - Up = −accel / |accel|
  - East = magnetic × Up
  - North = Up × East

3. Expresses the target spot direction in the device's axes(DirectionInDeviceFrame)
  - Transforms the bearing (b) and elevation (e) into North/East/Up directions: east = sin(b)*cos(e), north = cos(b)*cos(e), up = sin(e)
  - Before that, converts the bearing to magnetic north by subtracting declination and adds CompassBiasDeg (0, by default)
  - Then recombines that direction with the 3 arrows from point 2: East*east + North*north + Up*up resulting in a vector (x, y, z)
  - Uses the formulas atan2(x, depth) and atan2(y, depth) to determine the vertical and horizontal direction angles
  - Uses the formulas fx * x/depth and fy * y/depth to determine the pixel offset of the calculated position relative to the center
